NICKCHAT WEBSITE AND ANDROID APP: STEP BY STEP
==============================================

Files in this folder
  index.html               Home page
  app.html                 The messenger (the "Open Nickchat" button)
  manifest.webmanifest     Makes the site installable as an Android app
  sw.js                    Lets the installed app open even with a weak connection
  icon-*.png, apple-*.png  App icons
  Nickchat-1.0.exe         Windows installer (the "Download for Windows" button)
  credits.txt              Credits

Do not rename or delete any of these files. Upload all of them together.


PART 1. PUT THE WEBSITE ONLINE (GitHub Pages, free)
---------------------------------------------------
1. Unzip Nickchat-Website.zip so you have the "website" folder with the files above.
2. Go to https://github.com and press "Sign up". Make a free account and confirm your email.
3. Sign in as ilhamkbrli915-lab. Press the "+" at the top right, then "New repository".
   - Repository name: nickchat   (exactly this, all lowercase, no spaces)
   - Choose "Public".
   - Press "Create repository".
4. On the new empty repository page press the link "uploading an existing file".
5. Open your "website" folder. Select ALL the files inside it (Ctrl+A) and drag them
   into the GitHub page. Drag the files, not the folder.
6. Wait until every file shows in the list, then press "Commit changes".
7. Open the repository's "Settings" tab, then "Pages" in the left menu.
   Under "Build and deployment", set Source to "Deploy from a branch".
   Set Branch to "main" and the folder to "/ (root)". Press "Save".
8. Wait about one to two minutes and refresh the Pages screen. A line appears:
   "Your site is live at https://ilhamkbrli915-lab.github.io/nickchat/"
   That is your website. For you it will be:
   https://ilhamkbrli915-lab.github.io/nickchat/
   Send that address to your friends. Your files will be at
   https://github.com/ilhamkbrli915-lab/nickchat

If something looks wrong: wait two more minutes and press Ctrl+F5. Make sure
index.html is directly in the repository, not inside another folder.

Faster alternative: https://app.netlify.com/drop lets you drag the whole folder in
and gives you an address at once. Free accounts may be asked to sign up to keep it.


PART 2. INSTALL IT ON ANDROID (works after Part 1)
--------------------------------------------------
1. On the Android phone open Chrome and go to your website address.
2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
3. Tap "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen").
4. Tap "Install". A Nickchat icon appears on the home screen.
5. Open it. It runs full screen like a normal app, with no browser bars.
   Log in with a nickname and add friends.

Each phone keeps its own chats. Use Settings, then Back up chats, before you
reinstall or change phone.


PART 3 (OPTIONAL). TURN THE SITE INTO AN .APK FILE
---------------------------------------------------
You can make a real Android app file from your website for free:
1. Go to https://www.pwabuilder.com and paste your website address.
2. Press "Start", then "Package for stores", then choose "Android".
3. Fill in the app name (Nickchat) and press "Generate".
4. Download the zip. It contains the .apk (install it on a phone) and a signing
   key file. Keep the signing key safe: you need it for every future update.
To install an .apk file on a phone, allow "Install unknown apps" for the app you
opened it from, when Android asks.
